Run this audit before every production submission.

- Subscription and billing
- Refer and rewards

## Pass Criteria

- Text contrast is readable without relying on opacity.
- Cards, inputs, dropdowns, and modals use theme surfaces and borders.
- Required asterisks and error text use the same danger color.
- Primary buttons, secondary buttons, and disabled states are consistent.
- Bottom tabs and headers have safe-area spacing on Android and iOS.
- Empty/loading/error states do not use light-only backgrounds.
- Media thumbnails, video intro, and profile photos are visible.
