# Stakeholder Engagement Plan

Last reviewed: 2026-08-01

## Stakeholder Groups

| Stakeholder | Interest | Engagement need |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sponsor/Product Owner | Business success, product direction, launch approval. | High-touch decision updates and milestone reviews. |
| Parents/Guardians | Safe learning, child progress, payments, controls. | Clear UX, trust, reporting, consent, support. |
| Eligible students | Independent learning, schedules, AI tutor, assessments, progress. | Simple onboarding, engaging learning, privacy clarity. |
| Education organizations | CRM operations, admissions, communications, revenue, reports. | Reliable organization CRM, role controls, data import/export, support. |
| Counselors/admissions teams | Leads, applications, tasks, calls, follow-ups. | Efficient CRM workflows, filters, notes, assignments, reminders. |
| Marketing teams | Campaigns, source ROI, WhatsApp/email/SMS, lead capture. | Attribution, templates, approvals, delivery reports. |
| Finance teams | Payments, invoices, refunds, ledgers, reconciliation. | Accurate reports, exports, audit trail, settlement matching. |
| Tutors/mentors | Sessions, notes, attendance, assignments, progress. | Tutor workflows and classroom tools. |
| Support/admin teams | User help, account issues, safety, audit, operations. | Admin tools, logs, escalation and support procedures. |
| Security/legal/compliance | Privacy, consent, data retention, AI safety, payments. | Review checkpoints and evidence. |

## Engagement Strategy

| Group | Current engagement | Desired engagement | Actions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sponsor/Product Owner | High | High | Weekly status, go/no-go reviews, change approvals. |
| Engineering | High | High | Daily coordination, technical reviews, quality gates. |
| QA/Ops/Security | Medium | High before launch | Bring into staging planning and release gates. |
| Customer/demo users | Low/Medium | Medium | Demo scripts, feedback log, UAT sessions. |
| Parents/students | Low | Medium before launch | Usability testing, safety feedback, onboarding review. |

## Key Messages

- Mentora is build-clean and demo-ready, not live-production ready yet.
- Production readiness depends on provider activation, QA evidence, legal/security sign-off, and operations readiness.
- CRM and learning app are related but have separate user journeys and acceptance gates.
